Privacy

Your information.
A simple approach.

How this website handles the details you choose to share.

Using the quote form

When you select “Send quote request,” this website sends your name, contact information, service request, vehicle year/make/model, optional Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), damage details, and up to three optional damage photos to the shop via a Vercel-hosted server function and the Resend email service. Photos are resized in your browser before transmission and are sent as email attachments; they are not published on this website. The information is used to respond to your inquiry, not to confirm an appointment. If delivery fails, you may choose to send the message yourself using your email app.

Contacting the shop

When you submit the form, call or send an email, the contact information, vehicle details (including VIN if supplied), message and any photos you provide are available to the shop so it can respond to your request. The website does not display submitted details publicly. Avoid sending payment card details, identity documents, faces, license plates or other unnecessary sensitive information in your message or photos.

Cookies, analytics and maps

This website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Website images and assets are served with the site; photos submitted by customers are transmitted with quote requests, not published as site assets. The interactive Google map only loads after you select “Load map”; using it or the Google Maps directions link is subject to Google’s privacy practices. Your email provider also handles the messages you send through its service.

Hosting information

The website is intended for Vercel hosting. Vercel may process routine connection information such as IP address and browser details. Quote form submissions are processed by Vercel and Resend, which may retain data according to their applicable service policies. Shop staff receive the request in the shop email inbox.
