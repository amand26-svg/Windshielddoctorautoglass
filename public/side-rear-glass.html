Side and rear auto glass in Sarnia

Vehicle glass damage isn’t limited to the windshield. Door windows, fixed side windows and rear glass can all have different replacement requirements. Contact the shop with your vehicle information to confirm the glass and service availability.

Identify the window you need

  • Front or rear door glass: specify driver’s side or passenger’s side.
  • Quarter glass: a smaller fixed pane near the rear of the vehicle.
  • Rear glass: the back window, which may include heating elements or other features.

Before your appointment

Share the year, make, model and trim, along with a description of the damaged window. Let the shop know if the window no longer moves, if glass has fallen into the door or if there is damage beyond the glass.

Broken glass needs care

Keep people and pets away from loose fragments. If the opening exposes the interior or the damage affects visibility, call the shop about safe next steps before driving. Confirm glass availability before bringing the vehicle in.

Good to know

Before you visit.

How much will my auto glass service cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle, the glass required and the type of damage. Features such as cameras, sensors, heating elements and special glass can affect the quote. Send your vehicle’s year, make and model to get started.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call or email before visiting so the shop can confirm availability, the glass you need and the next steps. An email request does not confirm an appointment.

How long will the service take?

Timing depends on the damage, the vehicle, glass availability and any additional requirements. For replacements, adhesive curing also affects when the vehicle can be driven. Confirm the expected service and safe drive-away times with the shop.
