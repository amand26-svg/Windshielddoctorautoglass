Windshield replacement in Sarnia

Some windshield damage cannot be repaired. If replacement is recommended, your vehicle details help identify suitable glass and any features that need to be considered. Contact Windshield Doctor Auto Glass on Vidal Street South to discuss your vehicle.

Glass that matches your vehicle

Windshields can differ by model, trim and equipment. Tell the shop if your vehicle has a camera near the rear-view mirror, a rain sensor, heated glass, a head-up display or other windshield-mounted features.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Which glass is recommended, and what is included in the quote?
  • Is the glass available, and when can the work be scheduled?
  • Does the vehicle need camera calibration after replacement?
  • When can the vehicle be driven, and what aftercare is required?

Camera and sensor considerations

Some vehicles require camera calibration following windshield replacement. Confirm the manufacturer’s requirements and how any necessary calibration will be arranged. Ask how any required calibration will be arranged when discussing your quote.

After your windshield is replaced

Follow the technician’s specific safe drive-away and aftercare instructions. Adhesive requirements, vehicle design and conditions can affect timing, so there is no single waiting period that applies to every replacement.

Good to know

Before you visit.

How much will my auto glass service cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle, the glass required and the type of damage. Features such as cameras, sensors, heating elements and special glass can affect the quote. Send your vehicle’s year, make and model to get started.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call or email before visiting so the shop can confirm availability, the glass you need and the next steps. An email request does not confirm an appointment.

How long will the service take?

Timing depends on the damage, the vehicle, glass availability and any additional requirements. For replacements, adhesive curing also affects when the vehicle can be driven. Confirm the expected service and safe drive-away times with the shop.
