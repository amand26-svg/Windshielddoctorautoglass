Repair, replacement, quotes and appointments. Here are some useful starting points for your auto glass questions.

Repair & replacement

Can my windshield chip be repaired?

Some small chips can be repaired. The location, depth, size and condition of the damage all matter. A glass assessment is needed to confirm whether repair is appropriate or replacement is recommended.

What if my windshield has a camera or sensors?

Tell the shop about cameras near the rear-view mirror, rain sensors, head-up displays or heated glass. Depending on the vehicle, windshield replacement may require camera calibration. Confirm the vehicle-specific requirements and service arrangements before booking.

Can I drive with a damaged windshield?

Damage that obstructs your view or compromises the glass should be assessed before driving. A photo or online guide cannot confirm that your vehicle is safe to drive. If visibility is affected or glass is loose, contact the shop about safe next steps.

Quotes & appointments

How much will my auto glass service cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle, the glass required and the type of damage. Features such as cameras, sensors, heating elements and special glass can affect the quote. Send your vehicle’s year, make and model to get started.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call or email before visiting so the shop can confirm availability, the glass you need and the next steps. An email request does not confirm an appointment.

Will my insurance cover the work?

Coverage depends on your policy, deductible and insurer. Check with your insurer and ask the shop about payment and claim arrangements before authorizing work. Coverage and direct billing are not guaranteed.

How long will the service take?

Timing depends on the damage, the vehicle, glass availability and any additional requirements. For replacements, adhesive curing also affects when the vehicle can be driven. Confirm the expected service and safe drive-away times with the shop.

Can I send photos of the damage?

Yes. Submit a quote request with your vehicle’s year, make and model and VIN (if available). You can attach up to three photos to your quote request, including a close-up and a wider photo of the damage. Photos can help, but an in-person assessment may still be needed.
